      Why did my bill increase this month?

      Illustrative static example

      What shaped the answer

      The bill increased by $13.40. The statement shows a $5.00 monthly plan-rate adjustment and $8.40 in out-of-plan usage.

      Business proposal — RFP response review

      Review proposal claims before submission

      Which proposal claims are supported by approved past-performance evidence?

      Illustrative static example

      Fictional IT service-platform migration

      Claim review matrix

      Supported The team has completed three comparable service-platform migrations.
      Evidence result
      Two approved case studies support comparable delivery experience.
      Sources
      approved-case-study-alpha.pdf and approved-case-study-beta.pdf
      Required action
      Keep the claim attached to both approved case studies.
      Needs qualification The migration can be completed within a six-week transition timeline.
      Evidence result
      The approved transition plan supports an eight-week baseline; no approved source supports six weeks.
      Source
      transition-plan.pdf
      Required action
      Qualify the timeline or obtain approved six-week evidence.
      Unsupported The migration will cause zero service disruption.
      Evidence result
      No approved artifact establishes a zero-disruption guarantee.
      Source reviewed
      draft-technical-proposal.pdf
      Required action
      Remove the guarantee or replace it with bounded transition language.
      Evidence gap The transition team provides 24/7 cutover support.
      Evidence result
      The draft makes the claim, but the approved evidence set contains no staffing or coverage record.
      Requirement source
      rfp-requirements.pdf
      Required action
      Obtain an approved support plan or remove the claim.

      Review summary

      Evidence state counts

      • Supported1 supported
      • Needs qualification1 needs qualification
      • Unsupported1 unsupported
      • Evidence gap1 evidence gap

      Human proposal review remains required. These counts are not a readiness score, win probability, or submission decision.

      Evidence reviewed

      Public-safe fictional proposal packet

      • rfp-requirements.pdfClient requirements and cutover-support request
      • draft-technical-proposal.pdfClaims under review before submission
      • approved-case-study-alpha.pdfComparable migration evidence
      • approved-case-study-beta.pdfAdditional past-performance evidence
      • transition-plan.pdfApproved eight-week delivery baseline

      Illustrative receipt

      What is supported—and what still needs review

      Question
      Which proposal claims are supported by approved past-performance evidence?
      Supported result
      three comparable migrations
      Qualified result
      six-week transition timeline
      Unsupported result
      zero service disruption
      Evidence gap
      24/7 cutover support
      Decision state
      illustrative_static_example
      Boundary

      1. Proposal claim

      MNEMOS can help organize proposal claims and supporting evidence. It does not approve submissions or proposals, and it does not guarantee delivery timelines or service outcomes. MNEMOS does not replace proposal-owner, legal, commercial, or executive review.

      Why this matters: Teams can qualify or remove unsupported language while preserving the evidence behind claims that remain in the response.
