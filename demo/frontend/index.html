      Personal document understanding — bill

      Understand a bill without losing the source

      Audience
      People reviewing household or service bills
      Typical question
      Why did my bill increase this month?
      Documents involved
      Current and prior bills, rate notices, and account statements
      What MNEMOS shows
      Charge changes, the source lines behind them, and unsupported explanations that need review

      Example evidence trace

      1. Bill statement
      2. Charge comparison
      3. Source-backed explanation

      What MNEMOS does not claim

      MNEMOS does not provide financial, tax, or legal advice.

      Why this matters

      A clear source trail makes an unfamiliar charge easier to question or verify.
